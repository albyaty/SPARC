    How to join

    Institutions performing single-port robotic urologic surgery can join SPARC. Member centers' cases are included in consortium analyses, and members can propose and lead their own projects.

    01

    Active IRB at your home institution

    Approval to contribute de-identified single-port outcomes to the SPARC registry.

    02

    Data use agreement with the host

    A fully executed and signed DUA with Cleveland Clinic, the SPARC data host.

    03

    Prospective data on REDCap

    Ongoing case entry and follow-up on the shared registry.

    Cleveland Clinic Glickman Urological Institute

    SPARC was founded in 2018 by Jihad Kaouk, MD, Professor & Chair of the Glickman Urological Institute and Director of the Center for Robotic & Image-Guided Surgery. The institute hosts the registry, coordinates projects across sites, and publishes SPARC News, the consortium newsletter.
