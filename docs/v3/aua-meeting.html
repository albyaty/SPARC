Meeting format

Each meeting combines a research update, the Best of SPARC abstract presentations, and expert sessions on technique.

01

Research updates

Progress on active projects, open data calls, and new proposals from participating centers.

02

Best of SPARC

Short presentations of the year's top consortium abstracts, with live audience voting.

03

Expert sessions

Sessions on specific single-port procedures; in 2026, transvesical and low anterior access retroperitoneal surgery.
