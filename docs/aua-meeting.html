Meeting format

The meeting centers on the SPARC abstracts accepted at the AUA and on new single-port techniques, followed by updates on ongoing projects.

01

Accepted AUA abstracts

SPARC abstracts accepted for podium and poster presentation at that year's AUA, with discussion of the results. Selected abstracts are presented as the Best of SPARC, with live voting.

02

New techniques

Expert sessions on emerging single-port techniques; in 2026, transvesical and low anterior access retroperitoneal surgery.

03

Research updates

Progress on active projects, open data calls, and new proposals from participating centers.
