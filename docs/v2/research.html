Research

The SPARC registry collects outcomes of single-port robotic procedures from participating centers. Projects are proposed by members, reviewed by the steering committee, and analyzed on the pooled data.

How the registry works

SPARC is maintained through institutional IRB approval and data use agreements, with Cleveland Clinic acting as the data host and coordinating center.

  1. IRBEach center holds an active IRB approval at its home institution.
  2. Data use agreementA fully executed DUA with the host institution governs data sharing.
  3. REDCap captureCases are entered and followed prospectively on a shared REDCap database.
  4. Steering reviewProject proposals are reviewed by the data host and steering committee.

Registry databases

Each database collects procedure-specific perioperative, oncologic, and functional outcomes.

01

SP radical prostatectomy

Transvesical, extraperitoneal, and transperitoneal approaches, including customized techniques.

02

SP simple prostatectomy

Transvesical simple prostatectomy and STEP: discharge, retention, and trifecta outcomes.

03

SP partial & radical nephrectomy

Low anterior access, flank, retroperitoneal, and transperitoneal kidney surgery.

04

SP nephroureterectomy

Upper-tract urothelial carcinoma workflows, including comparisons with the ROBUUST collaborative.

05

SP radical cystectomy

Emerging multi-institutional experience in bladder cancer and urinary diversion.

06

SP pyeloplasty & reconstruction

Reconstructive urology, transplant ureter reconstruction, and selected unique indications.
