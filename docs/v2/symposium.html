Welcome from the course director

“This course brings together the leaders and innovators of single-port surgery from around the country as we share the latest tips and tricks, new indications, and expand on our hands-on practical skill sessions.”

“The continuing success and growth of this symposium represent our collective commitment to clinical excellence and surgical education, especially as we strive to improve the outcomes for our patients.”

Jihad Kaouk, MD, FACS, FRCS (Glasg) Professor & Chair, Glickman Urological Institute
Director, Center for Robotic & Image-Guided Surgery, Cleveland Clinic

Program

Friday is a full day of didactic sessions and debates in the InterContinental amphitheater. Saturday moves to the Simulation & Advanced Skills Center for small-group, hands-on training.

Times are Eastern. Program reflects the printed 2026 agenda and may change.

Faculty

Faculty members and proctors from academic and community programs across the United States and Europe.
