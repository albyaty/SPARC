Single Port Advanced Research Consortium

Building the evidence for single‑port robotic surgery.

SPARC is the largest collaborative research initiative in single-port robotic surgery: a multi-institutional registry that pools outcomes from high-volume centers across the United States to answer the questions no single program can answer alone.

About SPARC

A shared registry for a rapidly evolving platform.

Founded in 2018 by Jihad Kaouk, MD, at Cleveland Clinic, SPARC brings together surgeons who use the purpose-built single-port robotic platform in urology. Participating centers contribute prospectively collected outcomes to a common database, so that technique, safety, and patient selection can be studied at a scale no single institution reaches.

  • IRB-approved, multi-institutional database with more than 4,500 enrolled patients
  • Prospective data capture on REDCap with steering-committee review of every project
  • 21 accepted or published peer-reviewed manuscripts and a strong presence at national and international congresses
  • 4,500+patients enrolled in the registry
  • 30+participating institutions
  • 21peer-reviewed publications
  • 17abstracts presented at AUA 2026

Meetings

Two meetings, one community.

SPARC convenes in the spring at the AUA Annual Meeting to review research, and in the fall at the Cleveland Clinic Single Port Robotic Symposium, where much of the network teaches the techniques behind the data.

Every spring · at the AUA

SPARC Annual Meeting

The consortium's yearly working meeting, held alongside the American Urological Association Annual Meeting. Investigators review active projects, plan new studies, and hear the Best of SPARC abstracts with live voting.

    Join SPARC

    Bring your center into the consortium.

    Any institution performing single-port robotic urologic surgery can contribute. Participation takes three steps, and members can propose their own studies to the steering committee.

    Contact the coordinating team
    1. Active IRB at your home institutionApproval to contribute de-identified outcomes to the SPARC registry.
    2. Data use agreement with the host institutionA fully executed and signed DUA with Cleveland Clinic.
    3. Prospective data collection on REDCapOngoing entry and follow-up maintenance for your center's cases.
