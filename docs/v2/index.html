Single Port Advanced Research Consortium

SPARC is a multi-institutional research consortium studying single-port robotic surgery in urology. Participating centers contribute prospectively collected outcomes to a shared, IRB-approved registry coordinated at Cleveland Clinic.

About SPARC

Founded in 2018 by Jihad Kaouk, MD, at Cleveland Clinic, SPARC brings together surgeons who use the purpose-built single-port robotic platform in urology. Participating centers contribute prospectively collected outcomes to a common database used for multi-institutional studies of technique, safety, and patient selection.

  • IRB-approved, multi-institutional database with more than 4,500 enrolled patients
  • Prospective data capture on REDCap with steering-committee review of every project
  • 21 accepted or published peer-reviewed manuscripts and 17 abstracts at AUA 2026
  • 4,500+patients enrolled in the registry
  • 30+participating institutions

Meetings

SPARC holds its annual meeting each spring during the AUA Annual Meeting. Each fall, Cleveland Clinic hosts the Single Port Robotic Symposium, where many SPARC investigators serve as faculty.

The consortium's yearly working meeting, held alongside the American Urological Association Annual Meeting. Investigators review active projects, plan new studies, and hear the Best of SPARC abstracts with live voting.

    Joining SPARC

    Institutions performing single-port robotic urologic surgery can join by completing three steps. Members can propose their own studies to the steering committee.

    Contact the coordinating team
    1. Active IRB at your home institutionApproval to contribute de-identified outcomes to the SPARC registry.
    2. Data use agreement with the host institutionA fully executed and signed DUA with Cleveland Clinic.
    3. Prospective data collection on REDCapOngoing entry and follow-up maintenance for your center's cases.
