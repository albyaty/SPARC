How to join

Any institution performing single-port robotic urologic surgery is welcome. Once enrolled, your center's cases are included in consortium analyses and your team can lead projects of its own.

Coordinating team contact Abdulrahman Al-Bayati, MD ala22@ccf.org Glickman Urological Institute, Cleveland Clinic
  1. Active IRB at your home institutionObtain approval to contribute de-identified single-port outcomes to the SPARC registry.
  2. Data use agreement with the host institutionComplete a fully executed and signed DUA with Cleveland Clinic, the SPARC data host.
  3. Prospective data collection on REDCapEnter cases and maintain follow-up on the shared REDCap database.
  4. Propose and lead projectsSubmit proposals for review by the data host and steering committee.

Coordinating center

Cleveland Clinic Glickman Urological Institute

SPARC was founded in 2018 by Jihad Kaouk, MD, Professor & Chair of the Glickman Urological Institute and Director of the Center for Robotic & Image-Guided Surgery. The institute hosts the registry, coordinates projects across sites, and publishes SPARC News, the consortium newsletter.
